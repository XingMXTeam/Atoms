# Quality expectations — polish

- Level: production-polished (D8).
- Rank: correctness of empty/loading/error/follow-up states > accessibility & interaction > visual craft & micro-interactions > performance > delivery docs.
- References: existing Atoms Studio home/workbench/share surfaces — calm Chinese typography, card density, tab chrome, QA badge language — refined rather than redesigned.
- Anti-references: marketing-site flashiness, motion that ignores `prefers-reduced-motion`, clipped project-card text, unlabelled iframe/tabs, external font/CDN flash causing CLS.
- Red lines: clipped 我的项目 card text at 1280/375; motion that continues under reduced-motion; runtime hits to forbidden CDNs; missing `演示回放` / `已达修复上限` / follow-up affordance; secrets or hiring-brief text in docs.
